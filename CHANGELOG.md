# Changelog

## v2.0.0

- The Language Hack is now Console Language, rebuilt as an Options Menu add-on (Options Menu v2.1.0 or newer)
- The game translation patches moved to their own add-on, om_game-translations
- The console's own Languages screen lists the stock languages, 日本語 and the downloaded ones on the SNES, NES, Famicom Mini, Famicom Shonen Jump and Super Famicom Mini
- Download more languages from the console: one at a time or all at once, with a progress bar; hold B to delete one
- Rōmaji titles on the Japanese systems, independent of the applied language

## v1.0.0

- Original Language Hack for the Shonen Jump firmware
